# CLIENT DELIVERY & ONBOARDING WORKFLOW: PRIVATE-LABEL SOURCING OPERATIONS

**Workflow Standard:** CDW-RFQ-2026  
**Audience:** Account Directors, Lead Procurement Engineers, Client Brand Owners  

---

## 1. PHASE 1: DISCOVERY & BILATERAL NDA SIGN-OFF (DAY 1 - 3)
1. **Intake & Scope Definition:** Collect initial industrial design files (STEP/IGES), 2D engineering drawings, target BOM cost, and target retail MSRP.
2. **Execute Master Confidentiality Agreement:** Ensure mutual non-disclosure and non-compete agreements are signed prior to CAD file distribution.
3. **Determine Regulatory Classification:** Confirm if product falls under FDA Class I/II or CE MDR Annex VIII rules to activate biocompatibility validation requirements.

---

## 2. PHASE 2: PARAMETRIC BOM & RFQ DISTRIBUTION (DAY 4 - 7)
1. **Configure Parametric BOM:** Input material grades (e.g., USP Class VI Polycarbonate, SUS316L), surface finish (Ra <= 0.8 um), and general tolerance standards (ISO 2768-m).
2. **Issue RFQ to Candidate OEM Roster:** Deploy automated RFQ via edge gateway webhook across 15-25 pre-vetted manufacturing facilities.
3. **Monitor Inbound Submissions:** Standardized webhook ingests vendor quotes, normalizes currencies to USD, and assigns lead-time competitiveness scores.

---

## 3. PHASE 3: SUPPLIER AUDIT & ANTI-COUNTERFEIT TOKENIZATION (DAY 8 - 14)
1. **Regulatory Sanction Screening:** Validate FDA FEI numbers, CE declarations of conformity, and ISO 13485 QMS certifications.
2. **Tooling Security Token Generation:** Issue cryptographic tooling serial hash (`TOOL-SEC-[HASH]`) for laser-marking on mold cavity base.
3. **Algorithmic Counter-Negotiation:** Dispatch formal counter-offer letters targeting client budget benchmarks and 30/40/30 milestone escrow terms.

---

## 4. PHASE 4: PILOT RUN, INSPECTION & COMMERCIAL HANDOVER (DAY 15 - 45)
1. **Golden Sample Evaluation:** Perform dimensional CMM inspection and ISO 10993 cytotoxicity validation on T1 sample parts.
2. **Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI):** Dispatch accredited 3rd-party inspector (SGS/TUV) for ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 Level II normal sampling.
3. **Escrow Tranche Release:** Authorize 40% milestone release upon zero critical defect certification.
4. **Final Delivery Pack Delivery:** Provide client with complete compliance dossier, bill of lading confirmation, and landed cost audit report.
