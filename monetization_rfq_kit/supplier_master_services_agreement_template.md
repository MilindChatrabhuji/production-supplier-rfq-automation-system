### 1. RECITALS & SCOPE OF ENGAGEMENT
1.1 The Buyer is engaged in the marketing, distribution, and commercialization of branded consumer hardware, diagnostic tools, and physical goods.  
1.2 The Supplier operates audited manufacturing facilities capable of producing tooling, components, assemblies, and packaging meeting the technical specifications set forth in individual Purchase Orders (POs) and Requests for Quotation (RFQs).  
1.3 This Master Agreement establishes the legally binding framework governing all manufacturing runs, tooling ownership, quality assurance SLAs, and payment milestones.  

---

### 2. TOOLING OWNERSHIP & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
2.1 **Tooling Ownership:** All molds, tooling, dies, jigs, fixtures, CAD drawings, firmware, and proprietary production data developed or paid for by Buyer are the sole and exclusive property of Buyer from inception.  
2.2 **Tooling Security & Marking:** Tooling shall be engraved with Buyer's unique asset identifier (e.g., `TOOL-SEC-[HASH]`). Supplier shall not move tooling to an alternative facility without prior written consent.  
2.3 **Anti-Counterfeiting & Exclusivity:** Supplier shall strictly refrain from producing, white-labeling, or distributing products or components derived from Buyer's tooling or design to any third party.  

---

### 3. QUALITY ASSURANCE STANDARDS & ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
3.1 **Inspection Protocol:** All production batches shall be inspected in accordance with ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 / ISO 2859-1 Level II Normal Single Sampling standard.  
3.2 **Acceptance Quality Limits (AQL):**
- **Critical Defects (Safety, biocompatibility breach, structural integrity failure):** **0.0%** (Zero Tolerance). A single critical defect rejects the entire batch.
- **Major Defects (Functional failure, dimension out of tolerance):** Maximum **1.0% AQL**.
- **Minor Defects (Cosmetic blemish, packaging scuff):** Maximum **2.5% AQL**.
3.3 **Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI):** Every shipment must pass formal 3rd-party inspection conducted by an accredited body (e.g., SGS, TUV Rheinland, Bureau Veritas) prior to dispatch.  

---

### 4. ESCROW PAYMENT MILESTONES & DISPUTE WITHHOLDING
4.1 Payments shall be governed by milestone escrow tranches:
- **Tranche 1 (30%):** Advance deposit upon PO execution and raw material procurement verification.
- **Tranche 2 (40%):** Released strictly upon receipt of verified PASS inspection report from accredited 3rd-party PSI auditor.
- **Tranche 3 (30%):** Released upon presentation of genuine ocean Bill of Lading (B/L), packing list, commercial invoice, and certificate of origin.
4.2 **Defect Remediation & Late Penalties:**
- Any unexcused manufacturing delay beyond agreed lead time shall incur a liquidated damages penalty of **1.5% of total PO value per week**, capped at 10%.
- Defective units exceeding AQL thresholds shall be replaced or remanufactured at Supplier's sole cost within 14 calendar days.

---

### 5. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE & GOVERNING LAW
5.1 **Clinical & Biocompatibility Compliance:** Where applicable, materials must strictly comply with ISO 10993, FDA 21 CFR 820, and CE MDR standards. Supplier warrants absence of toxic, non-compliant, or restricted substances (RoHS/REACH).  
5.2 **Dispute Resolution:** Any unresolved dispute shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) or International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) under UNCITRAL rules.
